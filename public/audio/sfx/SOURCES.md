# Dialogue blip sources

`dialogue-blip-a.wav`, `dialogue-blip-b.wav`, and `dialogue-blip-c.wav` are original synthesized sound effects for the XUEYING dialogue voice. They were generated with [jfxr](https://github.com/ttencate/jfxr) (synthesis source commit `e971e4c183beddaff3abcebb77f2eaae17a2b83b`), not sampled from any game or recording. The author states that sounds created with jfxr may be used freely, including commercially, without attribution; the jfxr code itself is BSD-3-Clause. The reproducible presets are in [`docs/research/dialogue-voice-presets.md`](../../../docs/research/dialogue-voice-presets.md).

All three exports are mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 44.1 kHz. Durations are 32 ms, 34 ms, and 30 ms. Their peak amplitudes are below 0.21, with short fades at both ends to avoid clicks.
